Limitations

  • Historical simulation on adjusted prices (a total-return proxy, not executable quotes). Dividends are assumed reinvested with no delay or tax.
  • Decisions are taken after a close and traded at the next session's open, with the stated slippage and commission. Market impact is not modelled.
  • Uninvested cash earns nothing. Taxes and withdrawals are not modelled.
  • The instruments were chosen today, with hindsight; funds that closed are absent.
  • One window (2005-01-03 to 2024-12-31, 5,217 sessions): the period in which every instrument used by any compared portfolio has prices. A different window can change every number and the ranking.
  • This is not a forecast and not investment advice.

How to read the terms

Time-weighted return
How the holdings performed, with the timing of contributions removed. The fair figure for comparing allocations.
Money-weighted return
The annual rate the money actually earned given when each contribution went in (an internal rate of return). Depends on timing as well as allocation.
Maximum drawdown
The largest fall from a previous high, in the time-weighted value. It is the worst loss someone who bought at the top would have seen.
Volatility
How much daily returns varied, scaled to a year. Higher means a bumpier ride, not necessarily a worse outcome.
Expected shortfall (95%)
The average daily loss on the worst 5% of days in the window.
Sharpe ratio
Average return above the risk-free rate per unit of volatility.
Drift
How far the actual weights wandered from the targets between rebalances.
Effective number of holdings
1 / sum of squared weights: how many equal positions the portfolio's concentration is equivalent to.
Diversification ratio
Average of the holdings' own volatilities divided by the portfolio's. 1.00 means mixing them did not reduce volatility.
